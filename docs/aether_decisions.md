# Aether decision register

Every design or process decision that the code, the guides or the benchmark rely on,
with its status and the evidence behind it. A decision that is still being measured
says, before the measurement runs, exactly what result decides it.

**Conventions.**
- A `CHANGELOG.md` entry that reverses a row says `Reverses D<n>`, and the row is
  updated in the same commit.
- A docs-only resolution of a silent-wrong behaviour (a guide caveat instead of a
  language fix) must say in its row why the language fix was rejected.
- Rows are never renumbered. A superseded row stays, marked superseded, with a pointer.

Seeded 2026-10-06 from the review's decision register. Status "accepted" means the
owner accepted the recommendation in the Resolution column on that date. Keys such as
W4-03 (work items), M2 (milestones), L1/Core-1/R-1 (releases) and G1 (guide passes) refer
to the maintainer's remediation plan of the same date.
