# D12 (role of `fx`): the FX-001 gate, W8-10

D12's pre-registered gate: run the `fx=warn` A/B only if FX-001 is at least 5%
of first-attempt failures.

## First attempts in the umbrella's tracked results

`Tests/aether_doc_bench/results/**/*.json` holds 304 first attempts (the
initial generation of each row, before any repair). They come from three
destinations, an ornith-35B (June-July runs, full and small guides) and two
local tiers (August, medium guide).

| reading | first attempts | pass | failing | of which no program | FX-001 first | FX-001 share of all failures | FX-001 share of failing programs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stored (the binary of each run, 2026-06-26-1 .. 2026-08-09-1) | 304 | 244 | 60 | 28 | 1 | 1.7% | 3.1% (1 / 32) |
| recompiled on L0 (2026-10-07-1), task files materialised | 304 | 247 | 57 | 28 | 1 | 1.8% | 3.4% (1 / 29) |

On L0, the 29 failing programs are 25 silent-wrong, 3 uncoded and 1 coded:
FX-001, from the ornith destination.

**Neither reading trips the 5% gate.** The `fx=warn` flag is therefore not
built: the plan builds it only when the gate trips, and that leaves rule #1 of
every guide alone.

## Context: the archived set's rejections

Every one of the 629 archived model programs compiled on L0 with no task
files: 319 exit nonzero. Their first codes are SCOPE-001 209, uncoded 73,
SYN-001 20, FX-001 5 (1.6%), ANN-001 3, other 9. This is not a first-attempt
population: it mixes repairs and seeds, and runs without the tasks' files
inflate SCOPE-001 and the uncoded count. It does not contradict the gate
reading.

## What the gate does not yet cover

- The W8-01 harness_out backup and W1's baseline board at the current stamps
  (B0) are not in this lane. D12's evidence column names the B0 failure
  histogram, so the gate should be re-read on B0 when it exists:
  `tools/aether_first_attempt_histogram.py --recompile` reads any results JSON.
- The tracked population is small: 32 failing programs, and the single FX-001
  is one model's. A 5% gate on 32 failures would trip at 2 FX-001s.

## Done regardless of the outcome (W8-10 action 3)

- `docs/aether_architecture_and_rationale.md` §5.6 now states the as-built
  relationship, checked against the binary. `fx` is a lexical fence for gated
  builtin call sites and is not transitive (a user function wrapping its own
  `fx` is callable anywhere). `@pure` is the checked guarantee, enforced by
  ANN-001 on fx blocks, effectful builtins and non-pure callees.
- Still for W6: the FX-001 message should carry the fix shape
  (`let x: T; fx { x = f(..); }`).
